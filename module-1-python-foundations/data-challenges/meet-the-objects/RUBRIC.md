# Rubric: Meet the Objects

**What you're graded on:** your one `.py` file, with Day 1 and Day 2 code and every written answer, submitted at the end of Day 2.

Point scale: Below Expectations = 1, Approaching = 2, Meets = 4, Exceeds = 5.
3 skills x 4 points = **12 points at Meets**, more with any Exceeds earned.

## Object-Oriented Programming (`object-oriented-programming`)

| Level | Pts | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds** | 5 | Meets, plus a comment explains what breaks in the two-list version when a station is added, not removed. |
| **Meets** | 4 | Step 1 names the stations with wrong counts, and `Station` has a dispatcher phrase beside each kept attribute. |
| **Approaching** | 2 | `Station` and `fleet` run, but no attribute has a dispatcher phrase, or Step 1 names no stations. |
| **Below Expectations** | 1 | `Station` does not run, or `fleet` is not built from the records in the file. |

## Functions & Modular Programming (`functions-modular-programming`)

| Level | Pts | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds** | 5 | Meets, plus predicts what an f-string of one `Station` shows, runs it, and names the method used. |
| **Meets** | 4 | `needs_restock()` reads from `self`, the two-station check moves one answer, and Step 4 says who reads each display method. |
| **Approaching** | 2 | `needs_restock()` takes bikes or capacity as parameters, or the two stations already disagreed before the change. |
| **Below Expectations** | 1 | `needs_restock()` is missing, or `__str__()` or `__repr__()` is never defined. |

## Collaboration (`collaboration`)

| Level | Pts | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Exceeds** | 5 | Meets, plus says what the rejected attribute list could answer better than the one the pair kept. |
| **Meets** | 4 | Step 2 answers 1 and 2 give a solo attribute list and what each partner argued where lists differed. |
| **Approaching** | 2 | Step 2 gives one joint list, with no solo draft or no record of where the lists differed. |
| **Below Expectations** | 1 | Step 2 answers 1 and 2 are blank. |
